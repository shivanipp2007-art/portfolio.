05Projects

Hands-on work so far.

Project 1

AIoT-Based Smart Street Light Network with Adaptive Energy Management

Overview

Intelligent streetlights that automatically adjust brightness depending on daylight, movement of people or vehicles, and energy requirement.

Problem Addressed

Conventional street lights stay at full brightness all night, even when nobody is using the road. This wastes electricity, increases costs, and adds to light pollution. This system saves energy while still keeping roads lit when needed.

My Role

Worked in a team on circuit design, wiring the ESP32 with the LDR, PIR sensor and LED, programming the control logic, testing the brightness response, and preparing documentation.

Skills & Outcome

ESP32LDR/PIR interfacingPWMIoT (Wi-Fi)Python & ML basicsTesting

Outcome: A working prototype that dims in low activity, turns on in darkness, and goes to full brightness when motion is detected, saving energy versus constant full-brightness lighting.

Project 2 · Sustainable Cities and Communities

Aim: To design and simulate a smart street light system that automatically adjusts LED brightness based on ambient light and motion, reducing unnecessary energy consumption.
